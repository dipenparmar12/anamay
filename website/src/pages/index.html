Who we are

A home for the Veda in the Himalaya

Anāmay means perfect, integrated health in enlightenment: a life free from disease.

Shree Anamay Nyas is a trust registered in 2006 in Uttarakhand for the training, development and support of Vedic tradition, knowledge and culture. Since November 2006 the ashram has stood on the Kausani Estate. Students from across North India train here as Vedic pandits in the Vedas, Upavedas, Vedāṅgas and Upāṅgas.

Our founder, Ashutosh Urs Strobel, came to the Himalaya at the request of his teacher, Maharishi Mahesh Yogi. His aim is to create again a Vedic atmosphere in the home of the Veda.

Dhyāna · Meditation

The quiet centre of every day

“Naturalness is the basis of effectiveness.” — Maharishi Mahesh Yogi

Students, teachers, volunteers and guests practise Transcendental Meditation together every morning and evening. It is a simple, effortless technique. The mind settles to quieter and quieter levels of thought, goes beyond even the subtlest thought, and experiences the inner Self, Ātmā.

TM is practised for about 20 minutes twice a day, sitting comfortably with the eyes closed. In 2013 the American Heart Association noted that it may be considered as an aid to lowering blood pressure.

Our founder

Ashutosh Urs Strobel

Born in Basel in 1954, he began yoga at the age of ten. In 1973 he left everything to go to Maharishi Mahesh Yogi.

Under Maharishi's guidance he researched EEG, taught Transcendental Meditation to thousands, and studied Rasaśāstra, the Veda and the Upaniṣads. He has lived in India full time since 1996 and became an Indian citizen in 2011. Today he leads the ashram he built from nothing in Kausani.

Kausani · the land of the Kauśikas

Where the first hymn of the Ṛg Veda was cognized

Tradition holds that the family of the seer Madhucchandas, who cognized the opening hymns of the Ṛg Veda, came from Kausani, near the source of the Koshi river. Mahatma Gandhi stayed here in 1929, before a panorama of Himalayan peaks some 300 km wide: Trishul, Nanda Devi and Panchachuli.
