Before you give

Anything else? Write to us through the contact form or call {{phones:inline}}, {{site:contact.hours}}.

What does the payment form ask for?

Your name, email, mobile number, address, city, state, pincode and PAN number, as on the payment form of our earlier website. Your card or bank details are entered on Razorpay's secure pages and are not stored on this website.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

No. We accept donations only, so cancellations and refunds are not possible. Our policies say more.

Who receives my donation?

Shree Anamay Nyas, the trust that runs Anāmay Ashram. It was registered in 2006 in Uttarakhand for the training, development and support of Vedic tradition, knowledge and culture.

Are there other ways to help?

Yes. You can learn Transcendental Meditation at a centre near you or in our 1-week course, or offer seva at the ashram. See all the ways to contribute.
