Built by Vāstu Vidyā

Rooms that face the mountains

Our buildings follow Vāstu Vidyā, also called Sthāpatya Veda: the Vedic science of architecture.

Every suite and room has its own bathroom and floor heating, so it stays comfortable through the winter. The larger suites have a small kitchen corner where you can cook for yourself if you like. The guest house stands on the north-facing side of the mountain, looking across the valleys to the high Himalaya.
