समदोषः समाग्निश्च समधातुमलक्रियः ।
प्रसन्नात्मेन्द्रियमनाः स्वस्थ इत्यभिधीयते ॥ sama-doṣaḥ samāgniś ca sama-dhātu-mala-kriyaḥ · prasannātmendriya-manāḥ svastha ity abhidhīyate

One is in perfect health when the three doṣas (Vāta, Pitta and Kapha), the digestive fire, all the tissues (dhātus) and all the excretory functions are in balance, with a contented mind and senses and the experience of the inner Self.

Suśruta Saṃhitā · Sūtrasthāna 15.41
How it works

Purify, then rebuild

How does one keep this state of perfect health? The sages' answer begins with purifying mind and body of impurities.

At Anāmay healing begins with Transcendental Meditation to purify the mind. Yoga āsanas and prāṇāyāma follow, with organic food and a daily routine that support the in-house Ayurvedic treatments and bring balance.

Our physicians read the pulse (nāḍī) to recommend treatment and diet. Our trained Panchakarma staff use hand-picked, in-house preparations made from organic herbs, free of additives. With fresh spring water and the clean Himalayan air, healing can be a joyful process. Everyone in the ashram follows this way of life, not only those who come for treatment.

An ancient system, still alive. Āyurveda is one of the world's oldest medical systems and remains one of India's traditional health-care systems. It combines plant-based preparations with diet, exercise and lifestyle. If you take medicines or have a health condition, tell your doctor about any Ayurvedic treatment. (NIH · NCCIH)
