Getting here

The road to Kausani

The ashram is at about 6,000 ft in the Kumaon Himalaya. The last stretch is a short walk through beautiful forest.

By air

The nearest international airport is Delhi. Nearest domestic airports: Pantnagar and Bareilly. From Pantnagar, take a private taxi to the ashram.

By rail

The nearest stations are Kathgodam and Haldwani (8 km apart). Three trains run daily from Delhi to Kathgodam, and two arrive in the morning, giving time to reach the ashram before evening. Shared taxis leave from Haldwani.

By road

About four hours by taxi or bus from Kathgodam. In Kausani there are two drop-off points: from the lower one it is a 2 km walk up, and from the upper road a 500 m walk down. Drivers can often take you to the upper point for a small extra charge.

Staying with us

The Anamay Vedic Retreat

Guests stay at the Anamay Vedic Retreat guest house, with 25 rooms, an Ayurvedic Panchakarma clinic and views over the valleys to the high snow peaks. The ashram and retreat are fully vegetarian. No alcohol or smoking is allowed anywhere on campus.

Kausani is often called the “Switzerland of India”. Mahatma Gandhi stayed here in 1929, and from its ridges a panorama of some 300 km of Himalayan peaks opens up: Trishul, Nanda Devi and Panchachuli.
