Where your gift goes

The young pandits, their teachers and the cows

Before 2020 the Vidyapeeth ran entirely on private donations. Government support now covers part of the Vedic classes. Donations still carry much of the daily life of the students and teachers, the Goshala, and the ashram's work to bring back rare Vedic traditions such as the Sāma Veda.

  • The students and their AcharyasThe daily life of the Vedic Vidyapeeth, our Gurukul for young pandits.
  • The GoshalaFresh milk twice a day and butter made daily. Caring for the cows is part of daily seva.
  • Rare Vedic traditionsThe ashram's work to bring back traditions such as the Sāma Veda.

Before you give

Anything else? Write to us through the contact form or call +91 98102 07429, +91 75993 67331 or +91 99172 48154, Every day, 9:30 am – 6:30 pm.

What does the payment form ask for?

Your name, email, mobile number, address, city, state, pincode and PAN number, as on the payment form of our earlier website. Your card or bank details are entered on Razorpay's secure pages and are not stored on this website.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

No. We accept donations only, so cancellations and refunds are not possible. Our policies say more.

Who receives my donation?

Shree Anamay Nyas, the trust that runs Anāmay Ashram. It was registered in 2006 in Uttarakhand for the training, development and support of Vedic tradition, knowledge and culture.

Are there other ways to help?

Yes. You can learn Transcendental Meditation at a centre near you or in our 1-week course, or offer seva at the ashram. See all the ways to contribute.
