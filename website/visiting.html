More about Panchakarma

Soothing and supporting treatments

  • AbhyangaWarm herbal-oil massage of the whole body, which nourishes the tissues and calms Vāta.
  • ShirodharaA steady stream of warm oil poured over the forehead, deeply settling for mind and nervous system.
  • ShiropichuA pad soaked in warm medicated oil is kept on the crown of the head.
  • ShirovastiWarm medicated oil is held for a while in a cap fitted around the head.
  • SwedanaGentle herbal steam that brings on sweating and loosens the tissues.
  • Local pain treatmentsTreatments applied to the painful area, chosen by the Vaidya for the individual.

The main detox therapies

  • VirechanaTherapeutic purgation: a guided cleansing of the digestive tract, classically for excess Pitta.
  • VastiMedicated enema, one of the principal Panchakarma procedures, especially for balancing Vāta.
  • VamanaTherapeutic emesis: a guided cleansing of the upper digestive tract, classically for excess Kapha.
  • NasyaMedicated oil or herbal drops given through the nose, classically for the head and neck.
  • RaktamokshanA classical therapy of letting a small amount of blood, used only for specific conditions and only when the Vaidya prescribes it.
