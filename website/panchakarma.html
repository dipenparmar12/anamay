पञ्चकर्म

Panchakarma: the fivefold renewal

The classical Ayurvedic way to cleanse the body of accumulated toxins, restore balance and rejuvenate.

What is Panchakarma?

Pañca, five. Karma, therapeutic action.

Panchakarma is an Ayurvedic form of therapy that eliminates toxic elements from the body: a therapeutic pentad.

The word karma here means “multifaceted therapeutic action” (bahu iti kartavyatā, Chakrapāṇi on Charaka Sūtra 2.15), and also skilful action (karmasu kauśalam). It is the science of metabolic detoxification (viṣaharaṇa), the art of bio-purification (śuddhīkaraṇa), and rejuvenation (kāyakalpa).

It is basically a bio-cleansing regimen. It prepares the body to absorb therapies better and brings the doṣas back into balance.

Objectives

Why Panchakarma?

Ayurveda offers a systematic, timely way to live a healthy and successful life by balancing the doṣas and eliminating toxins (āma).

स्वस्थ

Preventive

To maintain positive health in a healthy person.

रसायन

Promotive

To gain extraordinary qualities such as Rasāyana (rejuvenation) and Vājīkaraṇa (vitality).

चिकित्सा

Curative

To treat and remove the ailments of a person who is unwell. (Charaka Siddhi 1.53)

The journey

Three phases of a classical Panchakarma

Classical texts describe preparation, the main procedures, and careful aftercare so that the benefit lasts.

Pūrvakarma · preparation

Internal and external oleation (snehana) and gentle sweating (svedana) loosen accumulated doṣas and bring them from the periphery (śākhā) towards the digestive tract (koṣṭha).

Pradhānakarma · cleansing

The main procedures eliminate the doṣas by the nearest route. This is srotośodhana, the cleansing of the body's channels, from cell to system.

Paścātkarma · aftercare

Saṃsarjana karma: a graded diet and routine that rebuilds digestion and prevents the condition returning.

How it helps

The probable mode of action

Panchakarma is a tool for “bio-servicing”: it removes accumulated impurities and toxic metabolites, especially from the body's fluids.

१

Eliminates wastes

Removes metabolic wastes and toxins.

२

Clears the channels

Cleanses the micro and macro channels, from cell to system.

३

Repairs and nourishes

Helps early repair of tissues and improves nourishment and perfusion.

४

Relaxes and rejuvenates

Helps prevent early ageing and relaxes both body and mind.

५

Improves response

Increases the bioavailability of medicines and the excretion of what is not needed.

६

Prevents recurrence

Saṃsarjana karma aims at non-relapse and non-recurrence of disease.
