Seva, and a week to begin

Volunteering at Anāmay starts with a 1-week dive into Transcendental Meditation, and may lead much further.

Special opportunity for applicants

The 1-week programme

More than 3,000 people have written to join us as volunteers. We are a small ashram of six acres, so we created a special week that gives everyone a real opportunity.

During the week you learn Transcendental Meditation, yoga, the basic principles of Āyurveda, a general introduction to Maharishi Vedic Science and Vāstu Śāstra, and receive a free Jyotish consultation. Since October 2022 this week is required before volunteering at the ashram.

  • ₹5,000, all inclusiveA nominal fee for the full week. Every class must be attended. If you miss a class, the stay is charged at ₹900 per day with ₹1,300 extra for the week, and volunteering is not permitted.
  • Sunday, 4:00 PM to Saturday, 10:00 PMArrival by 8 pm is accepted. You are free to leave on Sunday morning. Extra days, if space allows, are ₹900. See the charges, daily schedule and dates.

After the week

Two long-term paths

For qualified participants in good mental and physical health, in collaboration with the international TM organisation.

Become a teacher of Transcendental Meditation

A 3–6 month Teacher Training Course. Graduates work with the international TM organisation on peace-creating projects in schools, colleges, universities and other parts of society across India.

A lifetime of spirituality and service

For those who wish to commit their lives to spiritual pursuits and service, and to Vedic studies and the Vedic technologies of consciousness. Open to those truly dedicated.

Volunteering

What seva at Anāmay looks like

We accept volunteers who can commit to at least two weeks and work about five hours a day. Three vegetarian meals and a morning Ayurvedic tea are provided (no coffee or black tea). Rooms are shared and gender-specific, with two to four people per room, each with an attached bathroom.

The climate is mild all year at about 6,000 ft, and snow is rare, so there is always work in the organic greenhouses. The cows need care, and construction and maintenance always need help. Skills in artistic wall painting, aroma oils and similar crafts are welcome.

Required: the twice-daily āsana and meditation sessions and the evening Āyurveda lecture. Please wait for confirmation before you arrive. We cannot accommodate unannounced volunteers or guests.
