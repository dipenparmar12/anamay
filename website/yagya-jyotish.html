Yagya

The archetypal act of creation

Vedic yagyas are a technology of consciousness that creates desired changes in the material world through Vedic sounds (mantras), performed in prescribed ways.

They connect the transcendent with manifest reality. Through yagya every aspect of the universe is reconnected to its source, and the world is nurtured and revitalised. Advanced students of the Vidyapeeth learn Kalpa, the hows and whys of these performances, and take part in them at the ashram.

Vedic mantras

Sound as the form of knowledge

The Vedas are transcendental knowledge captured in sound syllables, the mantras. Mantras are vibrations that exist forever in the unified field of natural law. Ancient seers cognized them and spoke them aloud. They hold the perfect correlation between name and form, and applied correctly they unfold the original form of knowledge in a person's awareness.

Jyotish · Vedic astrology

Reading the moment of birth

Jyotish is a Vedic science closely related to yagya. It reads the arrangement of the planets at the moment of birth and shows the opportunities and obstacles of a lifetime, and when events are likely to unfold. In India it is common to have a child's chart analysed soon after birth so that any remedial yagya can be performed early.

Yagyas may be performed for material gain or spiritual progress, or to remove obstacles on the path of progress. They may also be performed for success in an undertaking, for recovery from illness or surgery, or for another wish.
