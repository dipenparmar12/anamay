## Our Vaidya

Dr. Kuldeep Nautiyal (B.A.M.S) will be your doctor at Anamay Vedic Retreat. He received his 5 years of Ayurveda education from renowned Ayurvedic University in Uttarakhand, Himalaya Ayurvedic PG Medical College and Hospital. He has also been the Gold medalist in National and International Yoga Championships held in India. He has also mastered Yoga as education, and completed his study in Die-tics, Nutrition and Naturopathy. He has also completed his Panchkarma Certificate Course after graduation and done YTT 200 hrs classes. He has also learned and practice Kshar Sutra Technique of Ayurvedic Surgery for all Ano-Rectal diseases.

The legendary Ashtavaidyas were historically renowned for their mastery of all eight specialties of Ayurvedic Medicine, they have made the Vedic Ayurvedic system a rich and complete healing science. Ayurveda like all Vedic knowledge needs years of subtle, sustained and penetrating practical instruction from a teacher to truly gain real insight into all its aspects, this exhaustive and yet comprehensive knowledge has a history of being passed on in effectively in only such a manner.

Vaidya Kuldeep has been with Anamay Vedic Retreat since 2019 and has gained immense respect amongst his patients from all over Europe, America and other Asian countries. His honest uncompromising approach to all clinical aspects of the science of Ayurveda, his focus on rooting out the real cause of disease, as well as his superb diagnostic skills have achieved lasting results and relief for many. He continues to broaden his experience for dealing with the western psych and need for healing through exposure and travel to Ayurveda clinics in India. At thesame time he delves deeper into subtle research aspects with his peers in India, maturing into a trueand gifted health practitioner.
