Shree Anamay Nyas is a trust registered in 2006 in the state of Uttarakhand for the training, development and support of Vedic tradition, knowledge and culture. The Ashram has been situated in Kausani Estate since November 2006. For the last 16 years Anamay Ashram has been spreading the ancient knowledge from the Vedic tradition in the Himalayas by training students from all over North India in all aspects of Vedic literature including Vedas, Upavedas, Vedangas and Upangas to name a few.

Shri Pradeep Kumar Lohani Ji is the Managing Trustee of Shree Anamay Nyas. Shri Lohani Ji is an advocate at the Uttarakhand High court at Nainital. Shri Amarnath Joshi from Haldwani and Neeraj Chhimwal from Ramnagar are the other two trustees.

Shree Anamay Nyas is lead by the able leadership of Shri Ashutosh Urs.
