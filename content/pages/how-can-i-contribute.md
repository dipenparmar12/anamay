## How can I contribute to Anamay Ashram?

One simple way to contribute is to learn Transcendental Meditation at a center near you. You can also help by volunteering or by donating to the ashram.

## You are also welcome to help by Volunteering

[Click Here](../volunteer/index.html)

## तद्विद्धि प्रणिपातेन परिप्रश्नेन सेवया । उपदेक्ष्यन्ति ते ज्ञानं ज्ञानिनस्तत्त्वदर्शिनः ।। भगवद्गीता ४।३४

#### Know this: through homage, repeated inquiry and service, the men of knowledge who have experienced Reality will teach you knowledge. Bhagavad Gita 4.34

Sevā(service) in the Āshram is entirely based on the principle of the above shlokha!! Become a part of our lovely tradition that has its roots all the way down to time immemorial.

There are different kinds of services in the ashram from house keeping, gardening, to helping Vedic students and so on. Volunteers have supported the Āshram since it’s inception.

You can also help by donating to the Ashram. This donation will support the Vedic Pandits and the Goshala(the cow shelter).
