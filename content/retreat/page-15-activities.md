Anamay Vedic Retreat is located adjacent to Anamay Ashram in Kausani (Himalaya region, Uttarakhand, India) and has comfortable accommodation in addition to offering Vedic and Ayurvedic rejuvenation. It is also the ideal place for learning deep meditation and yoga.

The temperatures in the foothills of the Himalayas are moderate all year around. If you want to escape the the heat of the flatlands, Anamay Vedic Retreat is the perfect place to come to.
