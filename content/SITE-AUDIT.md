## Who the organisation is

- **Shree Anamay Nyas** is a trust registered in 2006 in Uttarakhand "for the training, development and support of Vedic tradition, knowledge and culture".
- **Anāmay Ashram** (also "Anamay Vedic Ashram") has been at Kausani Estate, District Bageshwar, Uttarakhand 263620 since November 2006, at about 6,000 ft.
- **Founder and leader:** Ashutosh Urs Strobel (Swami Ashutosh). He was born in Basel in 1954 and became a disciple and TM teacher under Maharishi Mahesh Yogi from 1973. He has lived full time in India since 1996 and has been an Indian citizen since 2011. His goal is "to create again a Vedic Atmosphere in the Home of the Veda, the Himalaya".
- **Trustees:** Pradeep Kumar Lohani (Managing Trustee, Advocate at the Uttarakhand High Court, Nainital), Amar Nath Joshi (Haldwani) and Neeraj Chhimwal (Ramnagar).
- **Vaidya:** Dr Kuldeep Nautiyal (BAMS, CKS, CPK), an Ayurveda, Kshar Sutra and Panchakarma specialist with the ashram since 2019. Dr Gunjan Nautiyal (BAMS) is the women's health doctor.
- **Name meaning:** *Anāmaya* means "fully integrated perfect health in enlightenment, free from disease". Its motto is पदं गच्छन्त्यनामयम् (Bhagavad Gītā 2.52), "arrive at a state devoid of suffering".
